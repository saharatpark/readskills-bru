Lesson Plan 1 · Unit 1

Previewing & Predicting and Identifying Main Ideas

📖 Code: 2031103 ⏱ 6 Periods (Weeks 2–3) 👩‍🏫 Asst. Prof. Naviya Chutopama

🎯 Core Concept & Objectives

Core Concept: This unit introduces basic reading strategies needed to understand academic texts. Students learn how to preview a text, make predictions before reading, identify topic sentences, and locate central main ideas.

Performance Goal: Complete the Vocabulary Preview and Prediction Quiz on the Web-Based Application with at least 70% accuracy.

🔍 6.1.3 Warm-Up & Strategy Key Concepts

Before reading any text, active readers don't jump directly into line 1. Instead, they Preview and Predict:

  • Previewing: Scan the Title, Subheadings, Visuals (charts/photos), and the first sentences of paragraphs.
  • Predicting: Ask yourself: "What will this text discuss?" and "What is the author's primary focus?"

📚 6.1.5 Vocabulary Preview (Academic Words)

คลิกดูการ์ดคำศัพท์สำคัญประจำ Unit 1 เพื่อเตรียมความพร้อมก่อนเข้าสู่บทความอ่าน:

Preview verb
การสำรวจเนื้อหาคร่าวๆ ก่อนอ่านจริง
"Good readers preview the text features to understand its organization."
Predict verb
การคาดเดาหรือทำนายเนื้อหาล่วงหน้า
"You can predict the author's argument by looking at the headline."
Topic Sentence noun
ประโยคใจความหลักของย่อหน้า
"The topic sentence is often located at the beginning of an academic paragraph."
Main Idea noun
ใจความสำคัญ หรือข้อความสรุปของบทความ
"Identify the central main idea without getting lost in minor supporting details."

Passage 1: Sleep and Academic Success

CEFR A2–B1
🖼 Figure 1.1: Memory Consolidation During Sleep Text Feature Clue
Getting enough sleep every night is essential for university students who want to improve their learning and academic grades. When students sleep for seven to eight hours, their brains process new lessons and store information into long-term memory. In addition, well-rested students can concentrate better in lectures, solve difficult problems faster, and feel less stress before exams. On the other hand, staying up late causes fatigue, poor memory, and difficulty paying attention in class.
💡 Tip: แตะประโยคแรกเพื่อเน้นสีเหลืองเป็น Topic Sentence (ประโยคใจความหลัก)

Passage 2: Urban Gardens in Modern Cities

CEFR B1–B2
🖼 Figure 1.2: Rooftop & Community Plots in Eco-Friendly Cities Visual Preview
Urban community gardens offer significant environmental, economic, and social advantages for modern metropolitan cities. These green public spaces absorb harmful carbon emissions, lower urban temperatures, and noticeably enhance air quality. Furthermore, local neighborhood gardens encourage sustainable agricultural practices and provide city residents with fresh, affordable food options.
🌿

Passage 3 (Story): The Secret of the Rainforest Expedition

CEFR A2–B2 Story
🖼 Figure 1.3: Maya analyzing Amazon Canopy Satellite Maps & Botanical Findings Story Visual Clue

Maya, a university environmental science student, had only forty minutes to prepare for her international research seminar. Instead of reading a four-hundred-page rainforest ecology report word for word, she skillfully applied previewing strategies to find key scientific findings quickly.

First, she surveyed the table of contents and noticed an intriguing chapter heading: "Uncharted Canopy Habitats and Rare Species in the Amazon Basin." By examining colorful satellite photographs and reading the opening summary sentence, Maya accurately predicted that the text explained newly discovered plant species capable of producing natural medicines.

Consequently, her smart reading habits enabled her to extract essential scientific evidence in just fifteen minutes, allowing her to deliver an outstanding presentation to her professors and fellow researchers.

💡 ฝึกเน้นข้อความ Topic Sentence ในย่อหน้าด้วยปุ่ม Highlighter ด้านบน หรือเริ่มทำแบบฝึกหัด 5 ข้อ

Reading Strategy Guide
Previewing and Predicting
Strategy 1 · Core Reading Skill

How to Preview and Predict (CEFR A2–B2 Strategy)

การฝึกสำรวจและคาดเดาเนื้อหาก่อนอ่าน (Previewing & Predicting) เป็นกลยุทธ์สำคัญที่ช่วยให้นักศึกษาอ่านบทความภาษาอังกฤษเชิงวิชาการได้เร็วขึ้นและจับใจความได้แม่นยำขึ้นถึง 40%

Step 1: Read the Title & Subheadings

ชื่อเรื่องบอกหัวข้อหลัก (Topic) โดยตรง ถามตนเองว่ารู้จักอะไรเกี่ยวกับหัวข้อนี้บ้าง

Step 2: Examine Visual Features & Bold Words

สังเกตรูปภาพ แผนภูมิ หรือคำศัพท์ตัวหนาที่เน้นย้ำประเด็นสำคัญ

Step 3: Read the First Sentence (Topic Sentence)

อ่านประโยคแรกของแต่ละย่อหน้าเพื่อดูทิศทางของเนื้อหา
